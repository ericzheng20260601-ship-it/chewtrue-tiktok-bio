Commission tiers (annual cumulative GMV)

GMV under USD 10,00010%
USD 10,000 ≤ GMV < 20,00011%
USD 20,000 ≤ GMV < 30,00012%
Every further USD 10,000 in GMV+1%
Maximum rate (cap)20%

Both KOL and KOC use these tiers. Within a tier, one single rate applies instead of splitting GMV across brackets. Above USD 30,000, the rate rises 1% per extra USD 10,000, capped at 20%.

GMV is calculated net of refunds and cancelled orders; those orders do not count toward the tier.

Tier upgrades are not retroactive. When annual cumulative GMV reaches the next tier, GMV generated from that day onward is calculated at the new commission rate; GMV generated before that day keeps the previous rate.

Annual cumulative GMV resets to zero on January 1 each year, so the new year starts again at the 10% tier.
